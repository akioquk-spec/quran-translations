Choosing an English Translation of the Qur’an

Six recommended translations for different reading needs.

A translation is a human attempt to convey the meanings of the Qur’an in another language. Different translations serve different readers, so this page offers six useful starting points rather than one single “best” translation.

01

The Qur’an

M.A.S. Abdel Haleem

Best for: Natural British English and smooth everyday reading.

Read on Quran.com
02

The Majestic Quran

Dr Musharraf Hussain

Best for: Families and guided reading, with clear headings and accessible English.

Open the online reader
03

The Clear Quran

Dr Mustafa Khattab

Best for: Beginners, young people and clear modern English.

Official apps & reader
04

The Noble Qur’an

Al-Hilali & Muhsin Khan

Best for: Traditional explanatory reference with extensive added notes.

Read on QuranEnc
05

Towards Understanding the Ever-Glorious Qur’an

Dr Muhammad Mahmoud Ghali

Best for: Careful comparison of Qur’anic wording and deeper study.

Open the translation
06

The Gracious Quran

Dr Ahmad Zaki Hammad

Best for: Contemporary English with strong attention to context and explanatory additions.
